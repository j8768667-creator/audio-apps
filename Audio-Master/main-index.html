🎧 What is A/B Match?

The "Louder is Better" Trap:
Human ears naturally perceive louder sound as having "clearer highs", "richer punch", and "deeper bass" (known as Fletcher-Munson equal-loudness contours). When mastering, because limiting and gain boost make the track 4 to 8 dB louder, it is easy to assume the master sounds better simply because it is louder.

How A/B Match Solves This:
When you turn on A/B Match, Rack Master Pro temporarily bypasses all EQ, compression, and limiting, switching to your original unmastered mix — but automatically amplifies the unmastered audio to match the exact loudness of your master!

• A/B OFF: Listening to the Mastered Chain (EQ + Multiband + Limiter)
• A/B ON: Listening to Original Mix (Loudness-Matched)

How to use it like a Pro:
Toggle A/B Match back and forth. If your master sounds clearer, tighter, and punchier at the exact same loudness, you have crafted a great master. If the unmastered mix sounds more open and dynamic, you may be over-limiting or over-compressing.
