01 / Harmonic Architecture

1. Harmonic Progression Engine & Chord Voice Leading

Harmonic progression forms the structural backbone of every song. In Sonance Studio, each chord block represents an intentional harmonic station with dedicated root, extension intervals, bass register anchoring, and functional Roman numeral analysis.

How to Amend / Edit Any Chord:

1. Click on any chord card in Tab 1: Harmonic Chords & Voicings (or click the ✏️ Amend button).
2. In the Amendment Studio panel below, adjust the Root Note (C through B), Chord Quality (Major, Minor, Dominant 7th, maj9, m11, sus4, add9, dim7, etc.), and Bass Slash Inversion (Root, 1st Inversion 3rd in bass, 2nd Inversion 5th, or Pedal Point).
3. The studio immediately updates voice frequencies, Roman numeral degree, audio synthesizer playback, and AI prompt tags simultaneously.

How to Delete & Reorder Chords:

1. Click 🗑️ Delete on any chord card to remove it from the cycle. The studio safeguards against empty progressions and automatically re-indexes subsequent bars.
2. Use the ← / → reorder buttons on chord cards to shift chords earlier or later in the bar sequence.
3. Click + Add Chord at the top of the grid to extend your progression up to 8 bars.

Voice Leading Rules of Thumb:
  • Stepwise Movement: Keep upper voices (soprano and alto) moving by whole or half steps rather than jumping large intervals.
  • Common Tone Retention: Retain notes shared between consecutive chords in the same register (e.g. C in Am7 to C in Fmaj7).
  • Bass Inversions (Slash Chords): Use 1st inversion (3rd in bass) like C/E or G/B to create smooth walking chromatic basslines.
02 / Melodic Cadence

2. Topline Melodic Hook, Vocal Compass & Phrasing

Topline melodies create instantaneous listener recognition. The Topline & Vocal Compass tab enables you to balance singable pitch bounds with rhythmic anticipation and contour tension.

Tenor / Alto C3 - G4 Range

Ideal sweetspot for modern pop anthems and warm radio leads.

Soprano / Synth E4 - C6 Range

Piercing high hooks, expressive vocal runs, and soaring EDM leads.

Baritone Warmth G2 - E4 Range

Intimate singer-songwriter storytelling and deep jazz crooning.

Whistle / Riff A4 - E5 Range

High ear-candy riffs, flute flourishes, and hook motifs.

Contour Selection: An Arch contour creates natural tension by rising toward the golden ratio (measure 3 of a 4-bar phrase) before descending to resolve. A Wave contour suits evolving hypnotic flow tracks, while an Ascending contour drives builds before drops.

03 / Arrangement Dynamics

3. Arrangement Timeline & Timecode Architecture

AI audio engines rely on clear structural signposts. Sonance Studio translates your bar-based song arrangement into millisecond-accurate timeline markers.

The Musical Timecode Equation:

Seconds per Bar = 240 / Master BPM
Example: At 120 BPM = 2.00s per 4/4 bar. An 8-bar Intro = exactly 16.0s (0:00 - 0:16).
At 114 BPM = 2.105s per bar. A 16-bar Verse = 33.7s (0:17 - 0:51).

Recommended Song Section Profiles:
Section Typical Bars Energy % Instrumentation / Dynamics
Intro 8 Bars 25 - 35% Filtered ambient pad establishing tonal root, tape flutter, soft vocalise hums.
Verse 1 16 Bars 50 - 60% Walking bassline, light drum pocket, intimate lead vocal storytelling cadence.
Build-Up 8 Bars 70 - 80% Rising snare roll, high-pass sweep, ascending arps, 2-beat drop cut before hook.

04 / DAW & Hardware Workflow

4. Exporting & Routing Multi-Track MIDI in Your DAW

Clicking the ⇣ Export MIDI button in the top navigation bar creates a Standard MIDI File (SMF Type 1) containing multi-channel musical data ready for immediate playback in any Digital Audio Workstation.

Channel Routing Assignment:
  • Channel 1 (Track 1): Polyphonic Harmonic Chords
  • Channel 2 (Track 2): Bassline & Sub Frequency Anchor
  • Channel 3 (Track 3): Topline Melodic Hook & Lead Motif
  • Tempo Track: Master BPM & 4/4 Time Signature
Recommended Software Synthesizers:
  • Chords: Keyscape (Rhodes Mk I), u-he Diva (Juno-60 Pads), Serum (Warm Analog Saw Poly).
  • Bass: Spectrasonics Trilian (Moog Taurus), Serum (Clean 808 Sub), SubBoomBass 2.
  • Melody: Vital (Pluck / Lead), Omnisphere (Flute / Bell), Native Instruments Kontakt.
Step-by-Step DAW Import Guide:
Ableton Live:

Drag the exported .mid file into the Session or Arrangement view. When prompted "Import tempo and time signature data?", choose Yes. Unfold tracks to route Chords, Bass, and Melody to individual VST instrument channels.

FL Studio:

Go to File → Import → MIDI File. Select "Realign events" and "Add only". In the channel rack, replace generic MIDI Out channels with FLEX, Sytrus, or Serum.

Logic Pro:

Drag the .mid file onto new Software Instrument tracks. Logic will create distinct tracks for Chords, Bass, and Lead. Assign Retro Synth, Alchemy, or Studio Horns.

05 / AI Music Synthesis

5. AI Song Prompt Engineering: Vocal vs Instrumental Mastery

Generative music models like Suno AI (v3.5 / v4), Udio (v1.5), Stable Audio 2.0, and Meta MusicGen require structured conditioning rather than vague adjectives.

Vocal Song Performance Mode:

When creating a vocal song:

  • In Suno AI: Switch Custom Mode ON and Instrumental OFF.
  • Paste the generated Style Prompt into "Style of Music".
  • Paste the Timeline Arrangement Cues into the Lyrics box. Suno interprets bracketed headers like [Verse 1: Intimate female vocals] and [Chorus: Soaring belting lead] to command the exact sections where vocals enter, soar, or rest.
  • The negative prompt removes vocal distortion and pitch errors without silencing singing.
Pure Instrumental Mode:

When generating instrumental flow or DAW stems:

  • In Suno AI: Toggle Custom Mode ON and Instrumental ON.
  • Paste the Timeline Cues into the Lyrics box to force section drops without singing.
  • The generated Negative Prompt strictly excludes vocal tags: vocals, singing, lyrics, speech, choir, vocal chops to guarantee 100% clean instrumental audio.
The 3-Tier Multi-Style Blending Formula:

1. Primary Core Style (60 - 75%): Determines the groove pocket, drum cadence, and baseline soundscape (e.g. Flow State Deep Work or Liquid Melodic Flow).
2. Secondary Style (20 - 30%): Injects atmospheric texture, singing bowls, or cyberpunk grit (e.g. Meditative Ambient Drone).
3. Accent Flavor (10 - 20%): Adds sonic signature spice (e.g. Japanese Koto & Shakuhachi, Velvet Rhodes Mk II, or Muted Jazz Trumpet).

Platform Prompt Quick Reference:
Suno AI (v3.5 / v4):

Keep style tag prompt around 120-180 characters. Use comma-separated acoustic tags with BPM and key. Place structural and lyric directives inside square brackets inside the Lyrics field.

Udio (v1.5):

Use brackets around key descriptors [female vocals, soulful]. Set prompt strength to 50% and clarity to 30%. Use manual mode to guarantee exact chord progressions.

Stable Audio 2.0:

Requires descriptive acoustic prose sentences detailing instruments, microphone placement, spatial depth, and master quality. Set duration to match your timeline length.

Google MusicFX & Meta MusicGen:

Focus on seamless continuous loops, melodic contours, tempo, and frequency balance. Works natively with token conditioning.
