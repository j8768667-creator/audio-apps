Everlast is a continuously evolving sonic environment. These five complementary layers form its core: Base grounds, Harmony drifts, Enviro surrounds, Shards flicker, and Sparkle illuminates.

Choose any audio file, drag files in from Files, or record with the mic. Your sounds become part of the ecosystem. Cloud grinds a sound into a granular texture that swells with the wind. Scatter plays it like a creature, and the birds and owls answer. Bed crossfades it into a slow loop that breathes with the world. In key retunes it to the current scale.
