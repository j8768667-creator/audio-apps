02 · Arrangement settings

Auto-filled from what Splice Deck heard across your files — tweak either field if you want to steer it. Order below sets the sequence; reorder with the arrows. Each clip also has a length field (seconds) in step 01 — leave it blank for an equal automatic share of the song, or set a number to pin that clip's length; the remaining clips split whatever time is left. Every clip can also be set to "Layer onto" another clip instead of taking its own segment — it then plays simultaneously with that clip, sharing its timing and crossfades, rather than one-after-the-other. Segment and crossfade lengths snap to whole bars at the target tempo so transitions land on a downbeat instead of an arbitrary point.

"DJ filter sweep" rolls the highs/bass off the outgoing clip and opens them back up on the incoming one during each crossfade — like a DJ working the filter knob on a mix, rather than a flat volume fade. "Rhythmic pulse" adds a synthesized low thump on every beat under the whole song — it's audible on its own, not just a subtle glue, so it's off unless you want that effect.

Left at 6 (full range) clips get retuned exactly onto the target key. Turning this down makes big shifts sound less robotic, but a partial correction can leave two overlapping clips slightly out of tune with each other during a crossfade, which is more jarring than either an exact match or no correction — so only lower it if a specific clip sounds artificial, and consider 0 (no retuning) over a middle value.
