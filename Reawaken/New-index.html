Overall

Choose any audio file, drag files in from Files, or record with the mic. Your sounds become part of the ecosystem. New sounds are tidied automatically: silence trimmed, clicks softened, loudness matched. Play keeps a song or speech as it is, with a transport and a playlist, and the ambience eases under it. Transform melts it into the world: Cloud (granular), Freeze (one held moment), Stretch (slowed many times), Bed (slow loop), Scatter (like a creature), Swell (one long breath), Slice (fragments at its natural breaks) and Pulse (on the heartbeat). Choir finds the held vowels or notes and sings them on the current chord. Dissolve blurs a sound from recognisable to a wash. Songs with a clear tempo show it; Pulse, Slice and Scatter can play on the beat, and the heartbeat can follow the song. Resample records what you hear (or just your sounds) and adds it back as a new sound. In key retunes it to the current scale. Tap a slider's value to reset it. Planted blooms also borrow your Scatter sounds.

Presets

Ready-made worlds. Each sets the key, time of day, weather, the mix and which layers are on, and a few plant blooms. Your own sounds and the master volume are left alone.

My scenes

A scene remembers everything: mix, key, time, weather, drift, your sounds’ settings and your blooms. Load brings it back, Update overwrites it with what you have now.

Key

Drift moves the world to a related key every few minutes, gliding the drone across. Changing the key retunes the drone, the creatures, the blooms and any sound set to “In key”. Choosing a key locks it, so “New world” keeps it until you unlock it.

Composer

Biome writes a short piece in the world's key, with a theme that returns. The key may move in the middle and come home. The other layers follow along. Refresh writes a new piece with your current pace and length. Instrument and audio choices apply to upcoming notes. Minimise keeps the music playing.

Paint a sound

Time runs left to right and pitch rises upwards. Tone paints notes and Air paints breath and hiss. Snap to key keeps your notes in the world's scale. Press Play to hear the loop.
